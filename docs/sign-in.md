# Signing in to itch.io (paid games)

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

Free games download without an account. You only need to sign in to download
**paid games you already own**. Signing in uses your phone and a QR code, so
there is nothing to type on the handheld.

---

## Signing in

1. The first time you open Itch-io it asks **Do you have an itch.io account?**
   Press **A** to sign in, or **B** to skip. You can sign in later from
   **Settings → Account** (press **Start** from any screen).
2. A QR code appears, with a short code beside it such as `KX7T-4MPB`.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera. itch.io asks you to log in if
   you aren't already, then shows the same short code and what Itch-io is
   asking permission for.
4. Check the code matches the one on the handheld, then approve.
5. The handheld shows **Signed in as …** within a few seconds.

Can't scan? The address is shown in small text under the code; open it in any
browser.

A code lasts 10 minutes. If it expires, or you tap Deny by mistake, press
**A** for a new one.

### What Itch-io is allowed to do

Itch-io asks itch.io only for what it uses:

- your public profile, to show who is signed in;
- the list of games you own, to mark them and find their downloads;
- downloading the files of those games, and of free games.

It cannot buy anything, change your account, or see your payment details.

---

## Signing out

**Settings → Account**, then press **Y**. Your downloaded games stay on the
SD card and remain listed in Itch-io; only downloading paid games needs you
to be signed in.

You can also revoke Itch-io's access from itch.io's website under your
account's connected apps. Itch-io notices the next time it starts and signs
itself out.

---

## Upgrading from 1.0.x (API keys)

Earlier versions used an API key typed into Settings. API keys are no longer
used: on the first start after upgrading, Itch-io removes the old key from its
settings and asks you to sign in once. Your installed games, including paid
ones, are kept. Until you sign in again you can still play, delete and rename
them; only downloading paid games waits for the sign-in.
